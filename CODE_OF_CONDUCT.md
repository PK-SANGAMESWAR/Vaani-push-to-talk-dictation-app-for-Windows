# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
(CC BY 4.0), in short form.

## Our pledge

We want participation in Vaani to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive; assume good faith.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Be patient with newcomers; speech and Windows audio are full of surprises.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information, including voice recordings or transcripts, without permission.
- Sexualised language or attention, trolling, or other conduct that is inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this code,
and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem inappropriate. To report a
problem, contact the maintainer privately through the details on their GitHub profile
(https://github.com/PK-SANGAMESWAR) or via a private security advisory. Reports are handled confidentially.

This code applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project in public.
